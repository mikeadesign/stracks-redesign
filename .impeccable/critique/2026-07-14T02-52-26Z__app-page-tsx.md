## Anti-Patterns Verdict

LLM assessment: Doesn't read as AI-generated. Barber-stripe mixin, redundant CTAs, and copy voice feel like genuine craft. One leftover tell: Services.tsx "The Menu" eyebrow label doesn't match the shop's voice.

Deterministic scan: `detect.mjs --json app` returned `[]` — zero findings. Clean, as expected; doesn't catch either real issue below (semantic/interaction judgment, not markup pattern).

Browser evidence: no console errors, no overflow at 320/375/desktop, all images have appropriate alt text, visible :focus-visible outline confirmed, off-canvas links correctly tabindex=-1 when closed. Every sampled contrast pair cleared WCAG AA (lowest 5.04:1).

## Priority Issues

[P0] "Closed" renders in alarm-red on the shop's own closed day — Hours.module.scss:53-61. `.today` unconditionally applies `color: $red; font-weight: 700` to both dayName and dayHours, declared after `.closed`, so red wins over the intended neutral closed styling on a day that is both today and closed. Fix: scope red/bold to open days only.

[P1] Orphaned lit grid cell in Services on desktop — Services.module.scss:34-40. 8 services in a 3-col auto-fill grid leave an empty last cell that renders as a solid lit gold tile (grid gap background) with nothing in it — reads as broken/loading. Fix: add a 9th filler item, force 2-col, or suppress the empty cell's background.

[P2] "Call ahead" vs "walk-ins welcome" repeated without resolution — Hours.tsx:11-14 and Services footer. Never resolved into one clear rule.

[P3] "The Menu" eyebrow doesn't match the shop's copy voice — Services.tsx generic uppercase kicker next to copy like "A proper barbershop."

[P3] Possible dead whitespace gap on mobile between About and Gallery sections — flagged by one reviewer via screenshot at 375px, worth a quick visual check.

## Persona Red Flags

First-time visitor deciding whether to walk in: hit directly by P0 — checking hours on a closed day produces alarm-styled "Closed" at the highest-stakes moment on the page.

Distracted mobile user: no red flags found; menu, tap targets, click-to-call all verified at 320/375px with zero overflow.

## Minor Observations

- Footer nav duplicates header nav exactly; no "back to top" affordance.
- Hours contact list and map embed link to the same address via two URL formats — redundant but harmless.
- Gallery images have genuinely differentiated alt text.

## Questions to Consider

- If today is closed, should the Hours row have a highlight color at all, or is no color more honest than an accidentally alarming one?
- Is "appointments recommended, walk-ins welcome" the real policy or unresolved hedging copy?
- Should $red be banned from ever expressing state (today/closed), reserved only for the brand motif and CTAs?
